# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Everyday mobile-money users in Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire and Burkina Faso, on a phone (mid-range Android, often slow or patchy networks, French first, FCFA as the unit of account). They hold or receive crypto and want to turn it into mobile money (Orange, Wave, Moov, PI-SPI), buy crypto with mobile money, or swap between assets. They are using it to move real money for a real need, often one-handed, outdoors in bright light, and are not crypto-fluent. A second, internal audience uses the admin console on a desktop: operators who approve payouts, handle refunds and deliveries, and owners who manage rules and the team.

## Product Purpose

Relay is a crypto to FCFA exchange: sell crypto and be paid in mobile money, buy crypto with mobile money, and swap assets from the customer's own wallet. Customers keep custody of their funds until the moment they choose to sell. Success is a customer who completes a transaction without needing help, understands exactly what they will get and what happens next, and trusts Relay enough to come back. For the business, success is safe volume: every limit, hold, verification and approval is clear to the customer and enforced by the server.

## Brand Personality

Calm, confident, straightforward. The voice is plain and specific (one fee line, honest amounts, no jargon), reassuring without being stiff, and decisive without pressure. It should feel closer to a trusted local money app than to an exchange or a bank. Customers should finish a transaction feeling safe and in control, that it was fast and effortless, and that the product is a little more modern and polished than they expected.

## Anti-references

- A crypto-bro exchange: neon on dark, trading charts and jargon everywhere, hype.
- A legacy bank form: dense, grey, bureaucratic, intimidating, long forms with unexplained fields.
- A generic SaaS template: identical card grids, gradient text, hero-metric blocks, decoration without purpose.
- Scammy or get-rich-quick patterns: big promises, countdown pressure, flashy rewards, anything that rushes a money decision.

References that get the feel right: Wave (the mobile-money app many customers already trust: simple, bold, local) and Cash App (fast money movement with a confident, minimal feel).

## Design Principles

1. **Show the money before asking for commitment.** Every amount, fee, limit and arrival time is visible and exact on the review screen; nothing is discovered after confirming.
2. **One clear next step.** Each screen has a single primary action and says what happens after it. Waiting states (deposit, payout review, verification) explain what is going on and that the customer can leave.
3. **Calm under stakes.** Errors, holds and refusals are explained in plain language with what to do next, never blamed on the customer and never alarming. Urgency is never manufactured.
4. **Built for the real phone and the real network.** Small screens, one hand, daylight, slow connections, French first. Weight and speed are design decisions; the interface must stay usable when requests are slow or fail.
5. **Familiar before clever.** Use patterns and words customers already know from mobile money; reserve delight for the moment a transaction succeeds.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as the floor: text contrast at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text), readable in bright daylight, visible focus states and accessible names on every control. Honour `prefers-reduced-motion`. Must work at 360px width with comfortable touch targets, and with long French strings. Never rely on color alone to convey status (success, failure, hold). Language is French by default with English available; copy stays plain enough for low-literacy and second-language readers.
